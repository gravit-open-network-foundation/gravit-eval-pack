## 3. Consensus engine under Sybil attack (gravit-grover)

- 10x Sybil (100 honest vs 1000 Sybil nodes, 30 rounds): confidence in the true claim 0.9874, in the false claim 0.0126; Sybil stake share at round 9: 0.0006%.
- theta_critical calibration (3000 claims, seed 20261011): at theta = 0.73, TPR 91.7% (1376/1500), FPR 7.3% (110/1500), ROC AUC 0.856. The rates are identical for every theta in [0.11, 0.78]: after 30 rounds the threshold barely matters; what decides is whether honest evidence outweighs the Sybils. False accepts occur for hard claims, where honest evidence is weak.
- Merkle root of the 3000-record trace: `sha256:370326aa26d4cf7e5c87fa66e9809e6eeb451a29697ee7c9117b60d5599ac25d`.
